About the licensing column. ASCAP, BMI, SESAC and GMR don't publish who holds their licenses, so nobody can confirm it from the outside. The status is an estimate from how established the venue's music program is.

Before booking a covers set, ask the manager which PROs they license. Many hold ASCAP and BMI but skip SESAC or GMR, which changes what you can play. Original material sidesteps the issue.
